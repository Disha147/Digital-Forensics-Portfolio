CASE ID: DF-001

ANALYSIS

The examined Windows artifacts were reviewed and correlated
to identify recorded system activity during the investigation period.

Available evidence indicates:

1. Windows Security logs recorded credential-related activity.
2. A Special Logon event was recorded during the investigation period.
3. A successful Windows Logon event was recorded at approximately 9:59 PM.
4. The examined events provide information about recorded Windows
   activity during the investigation period.

The evidence does not independently establish:

1. The identity of the person physically operating the computer.
2. The intent behind the recorded activity.
3. That any particular file or information was stolen or transferred.

Evidence limitations:

1. The investigation was performed in a controlled laboratory environment.
2. Individual Windows events provide only part of the overall activity.
3. Additional artifacts would be required for stronger correlation.

Additional evidence that could assist the investigation:

1. Additional Windows Security/System/Application logs.
2. Browser artifacts.
3. USB/removable-device artifacts.
4. File-system artifacts and timestamps.
5. Network-related evidence.
