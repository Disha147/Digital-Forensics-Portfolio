CASE ID: DF-001

CASE NAME:
Suspicious Windows Activity Investigation

INVESTIGATOR:
Disha Ghosh

SYSTEM:
Windows 10

PURPOSE:
Investigate and document recorded activity on a Windows
workstation in a controlled laboratory environment.

INVESTIGATION QUESTIONS:

1. What recorded activity occurred?
2. When did the activity occur?
3. Which Windows artifacts can provide relevant information?
4. Was a removable device involved?
5. Was any program executed?
6. What websites or browser activity can be identified?
7. Can the available evidence identify the person responsible?
8. What are the limitations of the available evidence?
